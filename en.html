What FoglioPEF is

The models in the catalog are Excel files for monthly budget, mortgage calculator, net worth, 12-month cashflow, development appraisal, value-add, rentals, construction budget, treasury and DSCR, and partner split. You enter your own assumptions and compare scenarios. You can request a personalised business plan or financial plan on the Custom page.

Who it is for

Startups

For people preparing the numbers of a project — cash, funding need, scenarios — before talking to partners, a bank or advisers.

SMEs

For owners and managers who want budget, treasury and cash in a workbook they can check, without changing their accounting system.

Professionals and advisers

For people who support a business and want an Excel base they can adapt, with assumptions and formulas in view.

Why a FoglioPEF workbook

  • Transparent formulas and visible input cells.
  • Excel files, without macros.
  • Scenarios are compared from the assumptions you enter.
  • Models already for sale are in the catalog, with the list price.
  • If you need a different case, you can ask for a custom model.

Questions

Do the files use macros?
No, they are macro-free Excel files with inputs separate from calculations.
Can I get a custom model?
Yes, from €150 on the Custom page.
What do I get after purchase?
The Excel file, downloaded from the product page on Gumroad.
Are the models advice?
No. They are calculation tools: the results depend on the assumptions you enter.
What is the difference between Pack Base and the Rental Analyzer?
Pack Base analyses one property, with a budget and a 12-month cash flow. The Rental Analyzer compares up to 5 properties, long-term or short-term rental, over 10 years.
