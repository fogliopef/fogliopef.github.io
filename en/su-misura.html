Custom Excel models and financial plans

FoglioPEF Excel models built around the line items and data of your business or project.

FoglioPEF prepares Excel models for people who need to run the numbers of a property, a project or an SME's cash position. Every model follows the same rules: input cells separate from calculations, visible formulas, no macros, a dashboard with consistency checks and a user guide. The models are calculation tools and do not replace financial, tax or legal advice.

Questions

What is the difference between a business plan and a financial plan?
The business plan tells the story of the project and includes the numbers; the financial plan is the numerical part: forecast income statement, cash flows, balance sheet and indicators.
Is the plan suitable for a grant application?
The Complete tier is meant for a bank or a grant; the statements can follow the grant's template, if there is one. The outcome of the application depends on the body that reviews it.
When do the business days start?
From receipt of the complete data.
How many revisions are included?
1 for Essential, 2 for Professional, 3 for Complete.
Does the file use macros?
No, like every FoglioPEF model.
