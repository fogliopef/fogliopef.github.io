About FoglioPEF

FoglioPEF is a small project that publishes ready-to-use Excel models for personal finance, real estate and small businesses.

Behind FoglioPEF there is a team of expert professionals with many years of experience.

The Excel models in the catalog cover: monthly budget, mortgage calculator, net worth, 12-month cashflow, development appraisal, value-add, rentals, construction budget, treasury and DSCR, and partner split. The workbooks organise calculations and compare scenarios. A custom model is requested on the Custom page.

How we work

FoglioPEF prepares Excel models for people who need to run the numbers of a property, a project or an SME's cash position. Every model follows the same rules: input cells separate from calculations, visible formulas, no macros, a dashboard with consistency checks and a user guide. The models are calculation tools and do not replace financial, tax or legal advice.

We check each model before publication. The file includes instructions. When we prepare a new version, we publish the checked version in the catalog.
