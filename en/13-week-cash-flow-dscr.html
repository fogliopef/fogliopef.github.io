What a 13-week cash flow is

A week-by-week cash forecast for the next three months, built from the receivables and payables you already have, and rolled forward each week.

How DSCR is calculated

Cash available for debt service divided by debt service (principal plus interest) for the period. Above 1 means forecast cash covers the payments. A figure of 12,000 of cash and 10,000 of payments is a DSCR of 1.2: that is the arithmetic of the ratio, not a lender's threshold.
